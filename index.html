Fig. 01 · Restaurant performance · 2025 · Tableau, Excel

Restaurant Performance Dashboard

Three months of orders, revenue, customer ratings and staff data for a restaurant, reduced to one executive view: what sold, who sold it, and which days need explaining.

Notes on the figure

  1. Headline numbers first: 100 orders, $4,657.59 in revenue, a 3.9 average rating, 30 customers. The four figures a manager asks for before anything else.
  2. Daily revenue with the outlier days circled, so the conversation starts with the dates that break the pattern.
  3. Top sellers by volume. Caesar salad and the drinks outsell every pizza on the menu.
  4. Revenue by waiter, sized by contribution. This is where the gap in staff performance became visible.
Role
Framed the business questions, cleaned the data in Excel, modelled it as a star schema, designed the dashboard
Data
100 orders, 30 customers, February to April
Result
One menu category was driving 42% of revenue, and staff performance gaps were visible enough to back recommendations on pricing, incentives and scheduling.

Fig. 02 · Personal transactions · 2025 · Tableau, Excel

Personal Transactions Dashboard

Two years of personal transactions, cleaned and categorized in Excel and then modelled in Tableau to show where the money goes and how savings move over time.

Notes on the figure

  1. 806 transactions and $124,270 of income across 2018 and 2019, categorized before they reached Tableau.
  2. Two spikes, May 2018 and June 2019, that break an otherwise steady monthly pattern. The first thing to investigate.
  3. Category breakdown. A few categories account for 61% of non-essential spend, which is where the savings case was made.
Role
Data cleaning and categorization, data model, dashboard build
Data
806 transactions over 24 months
Result
Spending concentration identified in a handful of categories (61% of non-essential expenses), with projected savings improved by 15%.

Fig. 03 · Finance dashboard, in the browser · 2025 · JavaScript, HTML, CSS

Personal Finance Dashboard, as a web app

A single-page finance tracker with no backend: expenses, savings goals, investments, bills and a budget view, recalculated live from whatever you enter and kept in the browser between visits.

Notes on the figure

  1. Five views, one page: expenses, savings goals, investments, bills and budget.
  2. The category split is recalculated on every entry, so the chart is never stale.
  3. Every transaction is editable and persists in local storage, so the tool works offline and without an account.
Role

Data
1,200+ transactions and savings goals tracked
Result
